# Shooting Stars — first human playtest sheet (Summer build on your Mac)

The build at ~/shooting-stars on your Mac (Summer Engine) is the same
verified Godot build, ported 1:1. No human has played it yet — every
number so far is bot data. The chase is HELD (your word); this test is
board-only. Three short runs, ~15 minutes total.

## The fixed numbers your feel-verdicts will move
- Shot radii (levels 1–11): 19, 25, 31, 38, 45, 53, 61, 71, 80, 91, 102
- Spawn pool: levels 1–5, weighted small — [1,1,1,1, 2,2,2, 3,3, 4,4, 5]
- Kinds: HOT (blue) 40% / COOL (red) 40% / WHITE 20%
- Launcher at y=545; danger line at y=470 (settled shots only — a shot
  still bouncing from its own launch can't kill the run)
- Bot baseline: runs of 88 / 116 / 205 shots, scores 217 / 336 / 697,
  biggest shot 7–9. Typical ≈ 450 points — the chase constant is ~500
  points ≈ ~6 minutes when the chase is built.

## Run 1 — just play (no notes during)
Play one full run to OVERRUN. Afterwards, one line each:
1. Float: do shots rise too fast, too slow, or right?
2. Pace: is the wait between firing and the next shot ready too long?
3. Did any merge surprise you (a chain you didn't plan)? Did any merge
   you expected NOT happen?

## Run 2 — watch the readouts
Play again, glancing at the edges:
4. HOT / COOL / BALANCE (left): can you read it mid-play, or only when
   you stop to look? (Your rule: the UI should teach the game.)
5. The SHOT 1–11 column (right): did you ever look at it? Did it help
   you decide where to fire?
6. Danger: did you always know how close the pile was to the line
   before the run ended — or did OVERRUN surprise you?
